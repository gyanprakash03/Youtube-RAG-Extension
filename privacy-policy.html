VidMind Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 6, 2026

VidMind is a Chrome extension that allows users to ask questions about YouTube videos and receive AI-generated answers based on the video's transcript.

Information We Process

When you use VidMind, the extension may process the following information to provide its functionality:

  • The YouTube video identifier for the video you are currently viewing.
  • Questions that you submit through the VidMind interface.
  • Transcript content associated with the YouTube video.

VidMind does not request your name, email address, password, payment information, precise location, or other personally identifiable information as part of its normal functionality.

How We Use Information

The information processed by VidMind is used solely to provide its core functionality: retrieving relevant information from a YouTube video's transcript and generating answers to questions about that video.

Video transcripts are processed to create searchable representations that allow VidMind to retrieve relevant portions of the video when answering questions.

Third-Party Services

VidMind uses third-party services to provide its functionality. Information may be transmitted to these services as necessary to process transcripts, generate embeddings, retrieve relevant information, and generate AI responses.

These services include:

  • Supadata — used to retrieve YouTube video transcripts.
  • Google Gemini — used for AI embeddings and generating answers.
  • Qdrant — used to store and retrieve transcript embeddings.
  • Neon — used to maintain video processing and ingestion status.

VidMind does not sell user data or use user data for advertising, creditworthiness decisions, or lending purposes.

Data Security

Communications between the VidMind extension and its backend are made using HTTPS. API credentials used by VidMind's backend services are not included in the browser extension.

Data Retention

VidMind stores information required to provide its functionality, including processed transcript data and video ingestion status. VidMind does not intentionally retain user questions as a separate user profile or use them for purposes unrelated to providing the extension's functionality.

Changes to This Privacy Policy

This privacy policy may be updated from time to time to reflect changes to VidMind's functionality or data practices. Any updated version will be published on this page with a revised effective date.

Contact

If you have questions about this privacy policy or VidMind's data practices, please contact the publisher through the contact information provided in the Chrome Web Store listing.
